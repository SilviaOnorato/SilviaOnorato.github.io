## Hello everybody and welcome to my webpage!

I'm an astrophysicist working on the most distant quasars in the Universe. Bright beacons powered by supermassive black holes, they let us study the first billion years of cosmic history.

I am a post-doc at [Gemini Observatory](https://www.gemini.edu/), working with Ema Farina on high-redshift quasars (z>6) with JWST NIRSpec/IFU data, as part of the _Aether survey_. Before moving to Hawaiʻi, I did my Ph.D. at Leiden Observatory in the [ENIGMA](https://enigma-igm.github.io/) group led by Prof. Joseph F. Hennawi. There I used quasar spectra to study supermassive black hole growth, quasar lifetimes, and the intergalactic medium during the Epoch of Reionization. Even earlier, during my Master's at the University of Bologna, I worked on multiple stellar populations in globular clusters.

Want to know more? Have a look at my [Research](/research.html), my [Ph.D. thesis](https://home.strw.leidenuniv.nl/~onorato/thesis_webpage/), what I do in [Outreach](/outreach.html), or find out who I am outside of work in [About Me](/about_me.html)!
